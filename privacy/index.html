Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version: we collect the info you send us through the contact form and basic analytics about how people use this site. We use it to reply to you and to improve Rastro. We don't sell it.

Who we are

Rastro ("Rastro," "we," "us") builds commission tracking software for pest control companies that use FieldRoutes. This policy covers getrastro.com, including the demo at getrastro.com/demo.

What we collect

Info you give us. When you fill out the contact form, we collect your name, company name, email, sales team size, and whatever you write in your message.

Info collected automatically. When you visit the site, our analytics tool records things like:

  • Pages you view, buttons you click, and how long you stay
  • Browser, device type, and screen size
  • Approximate location (city or region, based on IP address)
  • The site or link that sent you here, including any ?ref= tag in the link
  • We may record how you move through the page (session replay). Form fields are masked.

If you submit the contact form, we connect your past and future visits to the email you gave us, so we know which pages you looked at.

On your device. We save your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage. Our analytics tool also uses cookies and local storage to recognize return visits.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message and follow up about Rastro
  • To understand which parts of the site are useful and which aren't
  • To decide what to build

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a few outside services to run this site. They only get what they need to do their job:

  • Formspree receives contact form submissions and forwards them to us.
  • PostHog provides our site analytics and session replay.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs (like IP addresses).
  • Google Fonts and the Tailwind CDN serve fonts and styles, which means your browser connects to them when the page loads.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or as part of a merger or sale of the business.

Your FieldRoutes data

Rastro isn't live yet, and this website doesn't connect to FieldRoutes. When the product launches, any FieldRoutes data you connect (customers, reps, sales, payments) will belong to you. We'll only use it to run Rastro for your company, and the details will be covered in a separate customer agreement.

How long we keep it

We keep contact form messages for as long as we're talking with you about Rastro, and analytics data for as long as it's useful for improving the site. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

  • Delete or see your info. Email cristian@getrastro.com and we'll send you what we have or delete it.
  • Turn off analytics. Add ?notrack to the end of any getrastro.com link, or use a browser setting or extension that blocks trackers.
  • Stop hearing from us. Reply to any email and tell us to stop.

Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU), you may have more rights over your data. Email us and we'll help.

Children

This site is for businesses. It isn't meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is a big one, we'll make it clear on the site.

Contact

Questions? Email cristian@getrastro.com.
